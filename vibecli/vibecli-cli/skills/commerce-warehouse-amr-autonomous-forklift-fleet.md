## What this machine is

The **Warehouse AMR & autonomous forklift fleet** is a non-humanoid autonomous machine whose job is to move pallets, totes, and racks and feed picking across the facility. A fleet of autonomous mobile robots and driverless forklifts coordinated by a fleet manager — complements human pickers and the humanoid warehouse associate.

## Operating-system context

This platform serves the *Commerce* operating system, whose mission is to match demand to goods and services, create satisfying experiences, and keep commercial operations profitable. It takes mobile and heavy-equipment work so people and the sector's AI agents can focus on planning, judgment, and exceptions.
